# Slice: Company Role Researcher

Research the target company, role context, and hiring contact. Output a 500-800 word Research Note with:
- Company/product context relevant to the role.
- Team/function context from the posting and public sources.
- One or two specific hooks for CV/letter writers. Hooks must be usable under rule 07 section F, which bans model and AI-vendor names (Claude, GPT/ChatGPT, Sonnet, Opus, Haiku, Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, Copilot, Ollama, LM Studio, LangChain, MCP, and similar) from application documents. If the company builds on one, phrase the hook vendor-neutrally ("your LLM-based assistant product") and flag that the brand cannot be named. The company's own legal name and product name stay usable verbatim.
- Likely hiring psychology and role pain points (label if inferred).
- Hiring contact if public and reliable; otherwise fallback salutation.
- Factual caveats (parent/subsidiary/legal-entity ambiguity).

Keep sources factual. No DOCX/PDF/LaTeX output.
