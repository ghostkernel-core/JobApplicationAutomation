# Rule 05 — Integrity & No-Fabrication Policy (STRICT)

The candidate chose the **strict** setting. This rule outranks "make it impressive."
A document that wins an interview on a false claim is a failure, not a success.

## The one test
> Every factual statement in the CV and cover letter must be **directly supported by
> `rules/00-canonical-profile.md` or the `/master` documents.**
> If it is not, it does not go in.

## Specifically prohibited
- ❌ **Invented metrics.** No "improved X by 15%/30%/40%" unless that exact figure is in
  the master. The current master CV contains **no percentages**, so the default CV/letters
  carry **no percentage claims**. (Older targeted CVs in `/Targeted` used numbers like
  15%/30%/20% — these are outdated and must not be reused.)
- ❌ **Skills/tools he doesn't have.** Only technologies listed in the canonical stack may
  be presented as competencies. If a posting wants something else, foreground the nearest
  real skill; optionally note willingness to learn (cover letter only, never the CV skills list).
- ❌ **Fake titles, employers, dates, degrees, certifications, clearances.**
- ❌ **The doctorate by default** — the current master CV does not list the Dr.-Ing.; do not
  add it unless the candidate explicitly confirms it for that application.
- ❌ **Overstated language levels** — German is B1+, not C1/native.
- ❌ **Inflated seniority** — don't call him "Senior" or "Lead" unless the posting title is
  that level AND the framing stays truthful about his ~5 years of experience.

## Allowed (this is tailoring, not lying)
- ✅ Reordering and re-emphasising real bullets to match the posting.
- ✅ Rephrasing a real achievement in the posting's vocabulary, meaning unchanged.
- ✅ Choosing which real projects/skills to feature.
- ✅ Adjusting role emphasis through real bullets, project order, and supported skills, without adding new CV personal-data fields.
- ✅ Qualitative impact language that is true ("enabling earlier identification of
  deviations", "supporting faster data-driven decisions") — these are in the master.

## Borderline → ask, don't guess
If tailoring to a posting seems to require a claim not supported by the canonical profile
(a specific tool, a metric, a seniority level, the doctorate), **stop and ask the
candidate** rather than inventing it. One quick question beats a fabricated CV.

## Verifier mandate
The CV verifier (agent 04), cover-letter verifier (agent 06), and final verifier (agent 09)
must each diff every claim against this rule and the canonical profile, and flag anything
unsupported **before** PDFs are generated.
